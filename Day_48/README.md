## Main topics

- The simple linear regression model
- Ordinary least squares and the best-fit line
- Slope and intercept interpretation
- Training and prediction with scikit-learn
- Residuals and basic model limitations

---

## Learning objectives

1. Write and explain the simple linear regression equation.
2. Derive the OLS slope and intercept for one feature.
3. Interpret coefficients in the units of the data.
4. Fit and predict with `LinearRegression`.
5. Explain why correlation is not sufficient for causation.
