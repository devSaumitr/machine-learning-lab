## Day number

**51**

---

## Main topics

- Why iterative optimisation is needed
- Cost functions, slopes, and the learning rate
- Updating slope and intercept together
- Contour plots and three-dimensional loss surfaces
- Gradient descent implemented from scratch

---

## Learning objectives

1. Explain how the gradient points toward increasing loss and why we step opposite to it.
2. Interpret the effect of a learning rate that is too small, suitable, or too large.
3. Update both the intercept and coefficients in one iteration.
4. Relate the OLS solution to gradient descent.
5. Diagnose divergence and slow convergence.
